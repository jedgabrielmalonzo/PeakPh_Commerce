About PeakPH

PeakPH is a small team of five outdoor enthusiasts who design and sell reliable camping gear — lightweight tents, compact emergency kits, and durable cookware — made for adventurers in the Philippines and beyond.

See products
Our focus

Quality, safety, and simplicity. We pick gear that works for real trips — whether it's a weekend in the mountains or an emergency at home.

Founded
By five friends who love camping

What we sell

Tents

Lightweight, weather-resistant tents for 1–4 people. Easy set-up and compact packing size.

Emergency kits

Compact kits with first-aid basics, tools, and survival essentials — designed for quick access.

Cookware

Durable cookware and utensils for outdoor cooking — efficient, lightweight, and easy to clean.

Our mission

To make safe and dependable outdoor gear accessible to Filipino campers and urban families who want to be prepared. We believe good gear encourages more people to explore responsibly and to be ready in unexpected situations.
